Format details

Tab-separated, team in the first column. Columns in order: team name, collector email, 1st visit feedback, 2nd visit feedback, 3rd visit feedback. One row per collector; the team name repeats on each row and groups them. The first header row (mentioning Collector email or visit feedback) is ignored. Blank feedback cells are skipped. Use Upload to open a .tsv or .csv file directly.

Timing

Times are in your browser's local clock, 24-hour. Leave a visit blank to send it right away. Keep this page open until the push finishes — it waits here for any scheduled times. Keep the tab in front if you can: browsers slow down timers in background tabs, so gaps can stretch to about a minute.

    Fallback: send from the tracker's own tab instead

    One-click bookmark

    1. One time only: drag this button to your bookmarks bar → Send feedback
    2. Press Send now. The tracker form opens in a new tab with your rows.
    3. In that tab, click the Send feedback bookmark. A panel shows each row being sent and a final count. Keep the tab open until it says Done.

    This sends right away and ignores the scheduled times. Rows already sent from that browser are skipped if you click twice.

    Console script

    1. Open the tracker in Chrome at ita.indivillage.com/Only_Google_Sheet_Business_Feedback/.
    2. Open the browser console. Windows: press F12. Mac: press ⌥ ⌘ J. Click the Console tab.
    3. Click Copy console script, paste it in the tracker's console and press Enter. It follows the gap and scheduled times. Keep that tab open until it prints Done.
